Original GATE (ICLR 2025)

Interactive language-generated questions elicit an informative specification of human preferences. The paper evaluates prediction on held-out human judgments.

Our proposed extension

Queries are selected for their expected ability to change a block-level preparedness decision; the system then composes interventions from constrained action primitives.

Verified outcomes, not persuasion

Physical simulation and human approval are distinct from behavior prediction. A simulated success updates a household only after an explicit confirmation event in this demo.
